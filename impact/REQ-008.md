## Risks
1. Idempotency key handling may be inconsistent across services.
2. Incorrect fee deduction could lead to over-payout.
3. Offer expiry timing mismatches client clock.
4. Missing account validation could cause payout to wrong account.
5. Integration testing gaps between lending and deposits services.

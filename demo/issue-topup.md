Top-up of an existing personal loan in the mobile app

**Business request (Lending product owner)**

Customers with an existing personal loan should be able to request a top-up from the mobile app. The customer sees
an indicative offer, accepts it, and the funds are paid into their account.

Why: today a top-up needs a branch visit or a call; customers drop out and we lose the business to other lenders.

What we know:
- It is for the mobile app first.
- The top-up and the remaining balance become one new loan; the customer has one monthly payment afterwards.
- The money goes to the customer's current account with us.

Please work out what we need to decide, then write the user stories and acceptance criteria.
